**Goal:** Close the remaining display gaps: the hypothetical "today's holdings over the past years" chart the spec asks for, correct combined returns when securities move between a member's own portfolios, and a detail view of one security on the TV.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-portfolio-viewer-design.md` §5 (hypothetical chart; time-weighted returns), §7 (`/tv`). Reviews of 4a/4b recorded the transfer and TV-detail gaps (`docs/STATUS.md`).

## Design decisions

- **Hypothetical chart:** today's quantities (all priced holdings in scope) × each day's close in base currency, over the page's range (MAX = the ten years of stored prices). Labelled "Hypothetical: today's holdings at past prices" with one sentence explaining it ignores buys, sells and dividends. Shown on the dashboard and on each portfolio page below the performance block.
- **Moves between own portfolios:** in the combined series (all portfolios), a `transfer_out` in one portfolio and a `transfer_in` of the same security, date and quantity in another portfolio of the same member form a move. A move changes no value, so neither leg counts as money in or out. Single-portfolio series keep counting transfers as flows (money did leave or enter that portfolio).
- **TV detail:** on card scenes, OK marks the first card of the page (instead of pausing; pause stays on the media key and the footer button, as in the family app); arrows move the mark; OK opens a full-screen detail with a one-year price chart (listing currency), value, day change, quantity and unrealized gain (privacy mode hides money and quantity); Back, OK or ▲ from the top row closes. The Android app asks the page `window.pvTvBack()` before leaving on Back, so Back closes the detail first. A mark or detail closes itself after 5 minutes without a key.
